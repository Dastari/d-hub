A calmer drawer and fixes for the known provider gaps.

- Email previews show only the message text, under a dotted rule. Sender, address, subject and date stay in the feed row and in the reader (`o`). Senders are shown by name.
- PageUp/PageDown scroll the preview without changing the selection. Ctrl+D/Ctrl+U still page the feed.
- Delete dismisses a message and marks it read, before you start typing a reply. Once you've typed, it deletes text as usual.
- The reply box starts one line tall and grows with your draft up to six lines.
- Invisible formatting characters common in marketing mail are removed before drawing. They could leave stray characters on screen in some terminals.
- iCloud keeps one sign-in per account instead of reconnecting on every poll.
- Teams edits and reactions update messages in place and no longer bring old messages back as new.
- Quoted Teams replies show the quote; files, cards and images each have their own marker.
- Office 365 replies keep Outlook's quoted original.
- iCloud and Office 365 account pages have a **Folders** row.

Real-account acceptance for Teams, Office 365 and iCloud is still pending the owner's sign-in. Still unknown: whether iCloud saves a Sent copy of replies. No license has been selected.
